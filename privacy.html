Sayvilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Sayvilo is developed by Ömer ÇEŞİM. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts, editing drafts and reading preferences are stored on your device. Sayvilo does not operate a cloud script service and does not upload your scripts to a developer-operated server. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

Bluetooth connection

If you choose to pair an iPhone and an iPad, Sayvilo exchanges scripts, reading settings and playback information directly over Bluetooth. Pairing credentials are stored in the device-only Keychain. Choose Forget Pairing in Device Connection to remove saved credentials. Keep Sayvilo visible on iPad. Once connected, the iPhone app can stay in the background to relay remote commands. If iOS terminates the app or you force-quit it, reopen Sayvilo to reconnect.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases through the App Store. Sayvilo uses StoreKit to verify access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's processing of purchase and diagnostic information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Apple settings.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. No account is required to use Sayvilo.

Support

If you choose to contact support, the information you provide, such as an email address and the content of your message, is used to respond to your request. Do not include private scripts unless you intend to share them. Support correspondence is not uploaded automatically by the app.

Deletion and control

Delete scripts from the library, remove pairing credentials with Forget Pairing, and manage device backups in system settings. Deleting a library script also removes its associated local editing draft. Backups can retain earlier app data according to your backup settings.

This website

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure the site under its privacy statement. These pages add no analytics or tracking scripts.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data handling change. The updated date appears above.

Apple Watch remote

Your paired iPhone relays Watch controls to your iPad. Script titles, reading settings and playback status are shared with your Watch using Apple WatchConnectivity. Sayvilo does not send these to a developer-operated server.
